Refund Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026 · Last updated: May 7, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with AgentShield. This policy explains when and how refunds are issued.

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your paid subscription, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial payment. This applies to first-time purchases of Pro and Team plans only.

To request a refund under this guarantee, email kshkrao3@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request" within 14 days of your purchase date. Include your account email address.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically on a monthly basis. If you forget to cancel before a renewal, we will consider refund requests made within 48 hours of the renewal charge on a case-by-case basis. Please email us promptly at kshkrao3@gmail.com.

Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time via the billing portal in your dashboard settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you retain access to your plan until then. No partial refunds are issued for unused time in a billing period after the 14-day guarantee window has passed.

Exceptions

Refunds will not be issued for:

  • Accounts terminated for violation of our Terms of Service
  • Requests made after the applicable refund window
  • Downgrade from a higher plan to a lower plan mid-cycle

Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method via Lemon Squeezy.

Contact

Refund requests or billing questions: kshkrao3@gmail.com
