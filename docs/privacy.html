Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026 · Last updated: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guruprasad J Rao ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use AgentShield ("Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and GitHub profile (if signing in via GitHub OAuth).

Usage and event data

The SDK reports security events (prompt injection detections, tool misuse, memory poisoning) from your AI agents. This data includes event type, severity, timestamp, session ID, and any message or metadata you choose to include. We do not collect the full content of your agent's conversations unless you explicitly send it.

Billing information

Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy. We do not store your credit card details. We receive subscription status and customer ID from Lemon Squeezy.

Usage metadata

We collect aggregate usage counts (events per day, events per month) to enforce plan limits and generate analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Service
  • To enforce plan limits and process billing
  • To send security alerts and notifications you configure
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve the Service based on aggregate usage patterns

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (SQLite), Cloudflare KV, and Cloudflare R2, all within Cloudflare's infrastructure. We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. API keys are stored as hashed values and are never retrievable after creation.

4. Data Retention

Event data is retained based on your plan: 7 days (Free), 90 days (Pro), 365 days (Team/Enterprise). Account data is retained for the lifetime of your account. Upon account deletion, your data is removed within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare — infrastructure and edge computing
  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing and subscription management
  • GitHub OAuth — optional authentication provider

Each third party has their own privacy policy governing their handling of your data.

6. Cookies

We use a session cookie strictly necessary for authentication. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email kshkrao3@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify registered users by email of material changes. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: kshkrao3@gmail.com
